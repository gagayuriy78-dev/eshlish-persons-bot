# ISHLISH PERSONS — promo film

## Director's treatment

- **Ratio:** 9:16 vertical (1080x1920). On-screen copy in Uzbek. Runtime 33.0 s, looping.
- **Concept — "Shisha zinapoya" (The Glass Ladder):** the six CEFR levels are stacked glass tiles; the film climbs them from a Telegram tap to the 1 000 000 so'm summit, then folds the ladder back into the brand mark.
- **Promise:** Test your English inside Telegram, climb A1 to C2, and compete for 1 000 000 so'm.
- **Identity:** soft-green system — pale green field `#F5FAF6`, ink `#173324`, primary green `#3B7954`, leaf green `#76BF91`, mint `#A5D7B2`, peach `#FDB48C`, coral `#F2705F`. Frosted-glass cards with a white top highlight. Display face Bricolage Grotesque (800), body Plus Jakarta Sans. No emojis, no icons that read as emoji.
- **Hero:** a glass phone frame showing the Mini App, and the glass level ladder.
- **Motion:** (1) masked line reveals and per-word punches; (2) virtual camera climbing a world (the ladder) and push-through cuts; (3) instrument motion — timer ring, rolling counter, drawn connection lines.
- **Arc:** snappy hook → calm, precise onboarding → accelerating climb → tense PRO beat (timer, heart lost) → warm social beat → competitive leaderboard → quiet, centered lockup.
- **Payoff:** the finale is the only full-frame ink (dark) field; the ladder collapses into a single ring around the wordmark — structurally different from every pastel scene before it.

## Shot 01 / 0.00–3.50 s / 3.50 s — "INGLIZ TILINGIZNI SINAB KO'RING"

- **Purpose:** Immediate hook: this is an English challenge, and it is yours to try.
- **Exact copy:** eyebrow "ISHLISH PERSONS · ENGLISH CHALLENGE" at 0.15 s; hero word "INGLIZ" at 1.35 s; line "tilingizni" at 1.70 s; line "sinab ko'ring" at 1.95 s; small tag "Telegram Mini App" at 2.40 s. All hold until exit.
- **Composition:** pale green field. Three glass blobs (leaf green, mint, peach) in the upper half; hero word "INGLIZ" 30vmin, centred, cropping slightly beyond the left/right safe edge; two supporting lines 9vmin below it, left aligned at the safe inset; tag lower third.
- **Artistic idea:** the app's green, mint, and peach arrive as physical glass drops before any words, then the hero word lands across them like a label on a jar.
- **Choreography:** 0.00 green blob snaps in (stiff spring 320); 0.45 mint blob snaps; 0.90 peach blob snaps (each overshoot 1.08). 0.15 eyebrow tracks in from letter-spacing 0.6em to 0.28em. 1.35 "INGLIZ" letters rise from a mask with 40 ms stagger. 1.70 and 1.95 the two lines unmask upward. 2.40 tag pill pops. 2.0–3.5 blobs drift slowly (parallax). 2.90 the peach blob begins to grow toward centre.
- **Transition:** 2.90–3.50 peach blob scales up and its outline becomes the phone screen of Shot 02; type exits upward with blur slice. Two mechanics: scale-through + type shear.
- **Assets:** CSS gradients only.
- **Sound:** music downbeat on blob 1; accents on blobs 2/3 are intended, unverified.

## Pre-code review

- Adjacent shots differ in background system (pale green → phone UI → ladder world → peach → mint → pale-green rows → ink) and mechanic (pop, UI typing, camera climb, counter/timer, network draw, row swaps, ring draw).
- Every shot has ≥1 change per second; opener changes every 0.45 s.
- Each shot ends with a handoff carrier (blob→phone, phone push, certificate flip, heart→ring, nodes→rows, row→ring, ring→blobs).
- Not a physical product: no photography needed; the hero is the app interface built as CSS glass.
